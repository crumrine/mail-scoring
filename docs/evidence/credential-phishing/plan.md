# Credential-phishing candidate and validation plan

The observed synthetic live example requested a password under an account-suspension threat but had neither the exact sensitive phrase nor the existing identity-mismatch evidence. The candidate adds a separate bounded, versioned signal requiring an explicit password request, account restriction, and an actionable HTTP link outside the sender host. Quoted/forwarded material, warnings and ambiguous links abstain. This is heuristic evidence, not proof of phishing; sender and link domains alone do not prove identity.

Before any previously observed regression or eighth-cohort prediction, compare this fixed candidate to deployed rules-11 using only the frozen TRAIN manifests and synthetic clinical controls; require zero legitimate quarantines. Then require all nine prior observed cohorts and frozen authored controls to have zero candidate legitimate quarantines. Only after candidate and Workers evaluator receive independent review may the frozen eighth be scored. The unchanged fresh gates require zero candidate legitimate catches, at least 50% recall and at least a 20 percentage point gain over rules-5; unavailable spam is a miss. The 600-per-class dated research sample and private exclusion limitations are recorded in the freeze. No live message or private data is committed.
